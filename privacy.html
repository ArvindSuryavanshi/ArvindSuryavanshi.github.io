Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how ReelEngine, the video publishing tool operated by the Mr Vice City Daily YouTube channel ("we", "us"), handles information when it connects to YouTube through Google APIs.

Who uses ReelEngine

ReelEngine is a private, internal tool. It is used only by the owner of the Mr Vice City Daily channel to upload videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public, and we do not ask other people to sign in to it.

What Google data we access

When the channel owner authorizes ReelEngine with their Google account, it requests these YouTube API scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload our finished videos and set their title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the connected channel's name, so we can confirm videos go to the correct channel.

We do not access any other Google data, such as email, contacts, files or viewing history.

How we use it

The data is used only to publish our own videos to our own channel. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than uploading and managing our videos.

Storage and security

The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in ReelEngine's local data folder. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and is never stored on a server we operate. Passwords are never seen or stored by ReelEngine; sign-in happens on Google's own page.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any Google user data to anyone. Data is sent only to Google's YouTube API, to perform the uploads described above.

Use of YouTube API Services

ReelEngine uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. ReelEngine's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or through Google security settings. Deleting ReelEngine's local token file removes all stored authorization data immediately. On request we delete any stored data within 7 days.

Viewers of our channel

We do not collect personal information from people who watch our videos. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to your use of YouTube.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
